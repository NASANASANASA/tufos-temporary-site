PRIVACY

Sighting Report Privacy Policy

Use of data

Reports are used only to organize, investigate, and cross-check sightings, and to contact the witness when clarification is needed.

Publication

Names, email addresses, and identifying information will not be published. Public cases are anonymized and precise addresses may be generalized.

Retention and deletion

Data is managed by the Society research team. You may request correction or deletion of your submission at contact@tufos.org.tw.

Media and attachments

If you authorize AI editing and publication, the report text (excluding the contact field) is sent to OpenAI for editing. AI output is saved as a private draft. The Society reviews and approves the report and attachments before publication. Without this authorization, the submission remains private. Do not include personal information or material you lack permission to publish.
